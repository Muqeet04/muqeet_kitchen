Our process

How a kitchen lands —— Five days on site

01

Design

Measured to the millimeter and modelled in photorealistic 3D — explore and refine your culinary space before a single board is milled.

02

The workshop

Bespoke cabinetry constructed and hand-sprayed at our own bench while your household routine continues completely undisturbed.

03

The fit

Coordinated Monday strip-out, structural carcass alignment, laser-templated stone landing, and brass integration across 5 focused days.

04

Handover

Soft-close mechanisms calibrated, natural stone surfaces sealed, dimmable architectural glow set — kettle on before departure.
